# Picture standard

Every picture shown in the app must meet this standard. When in doubt, reject.

- Avoid pictures with women in general.
- Certainly nothing racy.
- Nothing focusing on beauty or sexuality.

In the learner's own words: "avoid pictures with women in general; certainly nothing racy, and
nothing focusing on beauty or sexuality. instead recommend uploading your own picture that
matches the vibe"

When no search result passes, recommend that the learner upload their own picture that matches
the vibe of the word.
